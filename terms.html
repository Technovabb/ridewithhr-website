Booking terms

Clear terms, no surprises.

How booking a journey with H&R Executive Chauffeur Services works, from your first message to the end of your ride.

Last updated

29 October 2026

How booking works

  1. Send us your journey details through the enquiry form, WhatsApp or a phone call.
  2. We confirm the vehicle, the price and your pick-up time.
  3. Your booking is confirmed only when we confirm it. Sending an enquiry does not book a journey.

Prices

  • We price each journey after reviewing your request: the route, passengers, luggage and the vehicle that suits it.
  • Every airport arrival includes a meet-and-greet.
  • The luggage vehicle is an optional extra for journeys with more bags than the car can take.
  • Your price is confirmed with you before the journey is booked.

Vehicles

Your vehicle is confirmed for each journey, based on your passengers and luggage. Some journeys are carried out by a partner transport operator. If yours is, we tell you the vehicle and operator before your booking is confirmed.

Child seats

Tell us how many child seats you need when you book, and we will confirm what we can provide.

Payment

We accept card, cash and bank transfer. To pay by card, we send you a secure Fygaro payment link once your journey is priced; this website does not take payments. If your journey needs a deposit, we tell you when we confirm your booking.

Changes and cancellations

Please tell us about any change to your plans as early as possible, by WhatsApp or phone on +1 (246) 231-2679. Any cancellation charge that applies to your journey is set out when we confirm your booking.

Flight delays and waiting time

Add your flight number when you book so we can plan your pick-up around your arrival. Any waiting time included in your price, and any charge for extra waiting, is set out when we confirm your booking.

Contact

Phone or WhatsApp: +1 (246) 231-2679, or email renee@ridewithhr.com. Read our privacy notice to see how we use your details.
