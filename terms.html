Booking terms

Clear terms, no surprises.

How booking a journey with Ride With HR works, from your first message to the end of your ride.

Last updated

14 October 2026 (draft)

DraftH&R Transport still needs to confirm its cancellation, waiting-time and payment terms. They are marked “to be confirmed” below.

How booking works

  1. Send us your journey details through the enquiry form, WhatsApp or a phone call.
  2. We confirm the vehicle, the price and your pick-up time.
  3. Your booking is confirmed only when we confirm it. Sending an enquiry does not book a journey.

Airport prices

Airport transfers by car, all in, at the same price to or from the airport:

  • South CoastUS$100
  • East CoastUS$120
  • West CoastUS$125
  • North WestUS$150
  • + Luggage vehicleUS$75
  • Every airport arrival includes our compulsory US$40 meet-and-greet, which is already included in the prices above.
  • The luggage vehicle is an optional extra for journeys with more bags than the car can take.
  • Van rates and journeys to other areas are quoted on request.
  • Prices are in US dollars.

Vehicles

Your vehicle is confirmed for each journey, based on your passengers and luggage. Some journeys are carried out by a partner transport operator. If yours is, we tell you the vehicle and operator before your booking is confirmed.

Child seats

Tell us how many child seats you need when you book, and we will confirm what we can provide.

Payment

We agree the payment method with you when we confirm your booking. This website does not take payments. Accepted payment methods and any deposit are to be confirmed by H&R Transport.

Changes and cancellations

Please tell us about any change to your plans as early as possible, by WhatsApp or phone on +1 (246) 231-2679. Cancellation notice periods and any charges are to be confirmed by H&R Transport.

Flight delays and waiting time

Add your flight number when you book so we can plan your pick-up around your arrival. Free waiting time and any waiting charges are to be confirmed by H&R Transport.

Contact

Phone or WhatsApp: +1 (246) 231-2679. Read our privacy notice to see how we use your details.
