Where we take you

Across Barbados.

Grantley Adams International Airport is in Christ Church, at the southern end of the island. From there we take you to hotels, villas and homes across Barbados.

That includes the west coast (St James and St Peter, with Holetown and Speightstown), the south coast (Hastings, St Lawrence Gap and Oistins), Bridgetown and the east coast. Service area and journey feasibility are confirmed when you enquire.

Joining a cruise? We also arrange transfers to and from Bridgetown Port: see cruise port & seaport transfers.

Questions

Airport transfer questions, answered.

Is a meet-and-greet included?

Yes. Every arrival at Grantley Adams International Airport (BGI) includes a meet-and-greet.

What do you need from me?

Your airline, flight number, where you are flying from and your scheduled landing time, plus the number of passengers, suitcases and any child seats. For departures, tell us where you are flying to and when.

Can you take us to the airport for our flight home?

Yes. We agree a collection time with you and take you to BGI.

How much does an airport transfer cost?

We price each journey after reviewing your request: the route, passengers, luggage and the vehicle that suits it. We confirm the price with you before anything is booked.

How can I pay?

By card, through a secure Fygaro payment link we send once your journey is priced, or by cash or bank transfer. No payment is taken on this website.

What if we have a lot of luggage?

Tell us roughly how many suitcases you have, and tick the luggage vehicle option on the enquiry form if you need extra room for bags.

Which parts of Barbados do you cover?

We arrange airport transfers across the island, including Bridgetown, the west coast, the south coast and the east coast. We confirm the details of your journey when you enquire.

Arriving soon?

Arrange your airport transfer.

Send your flight and destination details by the enquiry form, WhatsApp or phone. We reply with your vehicle, availability and price, and nothing is booked until we confirm it.
