Airport meet & greet

A clearer arrival, from BGI to your door.

Send your flight and destination details before travel. For arrivals at Grantley Adams International Airport, the team coordinates the meeting point, welcomes you on arrival and moves you onward with the transport plan already agreed.

  1. 01

    Share your flight

    Add the flight number, arrival date, passenger count and approximate luggage.

  2. 02

    Receive confirmation

    H&R Transport confirms availability, assigned vehicle, meeting details, total price and payment method.

  3. 03

    Meet and move

    Connect at the agreed airport meeting point, receive luggage assistance and continue to your Barbados destination.
