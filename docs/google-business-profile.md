### What verification may ask for

Google chooses the method and it can change from one attempt to the next.

- **Video (common for service-area businesses).** Renee records **one continuous video**, with no cuts and no edits, on her phone from the verification screen. Google lists on screen what to show for her case. It usually includes:
  - the **place the business is based**: the surrounding street or area and, if there is one, a street sign or entrance (Google does not show this to the public);
  - **the vehicles and equipment** she works with, ideally with the registration plate or any branding visible;
  - **proof she runs the business**: a document in the business name (for example a business registration, licence or bill) held to the camera, long enough to read.
  Follow the on-screen list exactly, record in daylight, keep any document readable, and film it as one unbroken take.
- **Postcard.** Google posts a code to the address entered. It usually takes 5 to 14 days and may be slower to Barbados. The code (five digits) is typed into the profile's *Verify* page. Do not request a new postcard while waiting: each new request invalidates the earlier code.
- **Phone or text, or email.** Offered only sometimes. A code arrives and is typed in; never share it with anyone.

If a verification attempt fails, read the reason, fix it (for example a clearer video), and retry only after fixing. Repeated failed attempts can lock the profile for a while.

**When Renee confirms her hours**, add to the `LocalBusiness` block (example for Monday to Sunday, only with her real times):

```json

  "@type": "OpeningHoursSpecification",
  "dayOfWeek": ["Monday","Tuesday","Wednesday","Thursday","Friday","Saturday","Sunday"],
  "opens": "00:00", "closes": "23:59"
}]
```

**When her correct social links are known** (Instagram's handle in earlier notes was wrong, and a TripAdvisor page needs to exist), add them to the same block:


**Check after every change:** open each page's source or run the page through Google's Rich Results Test (search.google.com/test/rich-results) and confirm the name, phone and email match the profile.
