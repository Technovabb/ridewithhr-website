Privacy notice

Your details, handled with care.

How Ride With HR uses the details you share when you enquire about a journey.

Last updated

14 October 2026 (draft)

DraftH&R Transport still needs to confirm two points: how long enquiry details are kept, and who handles data-protection requests. They are marked “to be confirmed” below.

Who we are

Ride With HR is the customer brand of H&R Transport, a private transport business in Barbados. You can reach us by phone or WhatsApp on +1 (246) 231-2679.

What we collect

When you use the enquiry form, you can give us:

  • the service you need, pick-up and destination, dates and times, and whether it is one-way or return;
  • the number of passengers, suitcases and child seats, your vehicle preference and whether you want the luggage vehicle;
  • your flight number, if you add one;
  • your name, and your email address if you add it;
  • anything you write in the notes.

If you message or call us, we also receive your phone number and what you send us.

How the enquiry form works

This website does not store your enquiry. When you press Send on WhatsApp, WhatsApp opens with your details typed into a message to us. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp. Messages sent through WhatsApp are also covered by WhatsApp’s own privacy policy.

How we use your details

  • To reply to your enquiry and give you a price.
  • To arrange and carry out your journey, including planning around your flight.
  • To contact you about your booking.

We do not sell your details.

Who we share them with

Some journeys are carried out by a partner transport operator. If yours is, we share only the details that operator needs to carry out your journey, and we tell you the vehicle and operator before your booking is confirmed.

How long we keep them

We keep enquiry and booking details only as long as we need them to arrange your journey and to keep any records the law requires. The exact period is to be confirmed by H&R Transport.

Your choices

You can ask us what details we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete them, by contacting us on +1 (246) 231-2679. Barbados’s Data Protection Act, 2019 gives you rights over your personal data. The person who handles these requests is to be confirmed by H&R Transport.

This website

This site does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Its fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your device’s IP address when a page loads. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors’ IP addresses for security.

See also our booking terms.
