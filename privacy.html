Privacy notice

Your details, handled with care.

How H&R Executive Chauffeur Services uses the details you share when you enquire about a journey.

Last updated

29 October 2026

Who we are

H&R Executive Chauffeur Services is a private transport business in Barbados. You can reach us by phone or WhatsApp on +1 (246) 231-2679, or by email at renee@ridewithhr.com.

What we collect

When you use the enquiry form, you can give us:

  • the service you need, pick-up and destination, dates and times, and whether it is one-way or return;
  • the number of passengers, suitcases and child seats, and whether you want the luggage vehicle;
  • your flight details (airline, flight number, where it flies from or to, and its scheduled time), if you add them;
  • your name, and your email address or mobile number (you give at least one so we can reply);
  • anything you write in the notes.

If you message or call us, we also receive your phone number and what you send us.

How the enquiry form works

When you press Send enquiry, your enquiry goes to H&R’s own booking system, which is hosted by DigitalOcean in the USA, so our team can see it in the H&R app. It is also emailed to us through FormSubmit, a form-to-email service. If you give your email address, FormSubmit also sends you a short note confirming we received your enquiry. If neither can be sent, the page offers to send your details to us on WhatsApp instead; that message is sent only when you press send, and it is also covered by WhatsApp’s own privacy policy.

Reviews

If you write a review on our reviews page, we receive your rating, your review, the name you choose to show, your email address and, if you pick one, your journey. It is sent to H&R’s own booking system, which is hosted by DigitalOcean in the USA. We read each review before it appears. The page shows the rating, the review, the name you chose, your journey and the month; your email address is never shown, and we use it only to check you travelled with us or to reply to you.

A review we don’t publish is deleted 4 weeks after it arrives. A published review stays on the page until you ask us to remove it.

How we use your details

  • To reply to your enquiry, give you a price and, if you pay by card, send you a secure payment link.
  • To arrange and carry out your journey, including planning around your flight.
  • To contact you about your booking.

We do not sell your details.

Who we share them with

DigitalOcean hosts our booking system. We use FormSubmit to email website enquiries to our inbox, and Microsoft 365 for our email. If you pay by card, Fygaro, our payment provider, handles the payment through the secure link we send you.

Some journeys are carried out by a partner transport operator. If yours is, we share only the details that operator needs to carry out your journey, and we tell you the vehicle and operator before your booking is confirmed.

How long we keep them

We keep enquiry and booking details for 4 weeks, then delete them, except for any records the law requires us to keep.

Your choices

You can ask us what details we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete them, by contacting us on +1 (246) 231-2679 or at renee@ridewithhr.com. Barbados’s Data Protection Act, 2019 gives you rights over your personal data.

This website

This site uses Google’s measurement tag (Google Analytics and Google Ads) to count visits and see which adverts lead to enquiries. Google may set cookies for this and receives your device’s IP address. You can turn off personalised adverts in Google’s My Ad Center, or block cookies in your browser settings. Its fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your device’s IP address when a page loads. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors’ IP addresses for security.
